# UX flow — Weekly plan

**Status:** Draft

## Entries

- Regular or daily participants.
- Days and duration available.
- Minute budget for each chosen day.
- Critical materials.
- Temporary preferences or restrictions.

## Main path

1. The system proposes a balanced week that approximately fills the minute budget of each day with one or several activities.
2. Each card shows purpose, duration, preparation and reason.
3. The adult can change day, substitute or delete.
4. The system recalculates variety without locking manual settings.
5. A consolidated list is generated: already available, to be assembled and optional.
6. The plan is published for the family.
7. The weekly pack is downloaded with content, contributions, objectives, safety guidance, and images.

## Criteria

- **UX-301:** Replacing an activity does not restart the entire plan.
- **UX-302:** Missing materials are identified before the day of execution.
- **UX-303:** The adult can change participants on the same day.
- **UX-304:** Planning does not affirm certainty about availability of approximate inventory.
- **UX-305:** The number of activities is derived from time and is not an objective in itself.
- **UX-306:** The adult can choose 30, 60 or other supported budget without regenerating unaffected days.
