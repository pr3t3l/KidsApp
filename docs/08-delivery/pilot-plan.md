# Family pilot plan

**Status:** Draft
**Version:** 0.2

## Purpose

Validate that the system is useful, safe, trustworthy, and sustainable during a real afternoon—not merely that the software works.

## Founder pilot — eight weeks

- Use the system weekly for eight weeks with the founder's five-year-old daughter.
- First run 3 activities to calibrate the scheme and then expand to the pilot library.
- Record preparation, duration, failures and real roles.
- Adjust Activity Schema and independence scale.
- Confirm that one primary objective is sufficient.
- Maintain a short journal of editorial decisions and version changes.

### Initial calibration sequence

Before expanding the library, calibrate the scheme with the three activities from [Pilot Pack v0.1](pilot-pack-v0.1.md):

1. `ACT-0001` Paper bridge: first adult dry run without child participation; after reaching `ready_for_pilot`, first controlled trial with the founder's daughter and time measurement.
2. `ACT-0002` Seed Sorting: Run only after small parts, allergy and toxicology check; try separately with one, two and three children.
3. `ACT-0003` Conductivity tester: do not run with children until completing the reinforced electrical gate and adult physical tests.

A decisive review `rejected` returns the activity to the appropriate editorial state and also produces learning; `rejected` is not an ActivityVersion state. A new version does not silently replace the one referenced by a session.

## Small pilot

Confirmed proposal: Supplement the founder and her daughter's pilot with friendly families who have two or three children each, for part or all of the eight weeks, subject to appropriate privacy, consent and review. The exact number of participating families has not yet been set.

Diversity sought:

- One child in the founding family and sessions of two or three children in friendly families.
- Adults with different technical comfort.
- Different levels of inventory and space.
- Age range within the approved scope.

## Research questions

1. Does the preparation coincide with the announced time?
2. Do children accept and understand roles? Do they allow you to work together or do they generate waiting, hierarchy, competition or conflict?
3. Does the adult understand the primary objective?
4. Can you close in less than 20 seconds?
5. In the functional phase with `VS-05`, does the signal obtained improve the following recommendation?
6. Are explanations and images enough when something goes wrong?
7. What information does the adult consider comfortable sharing?
8. Does the product reduce or increase mental load?

## Minimum pilot data

- Activity/version.
- Participants and roles.
- Proposed role, actual role and optional reason for change, combination, observation or non-participation.
- Start, closing and approximate duration.
- Optional main assessment.
- Failure or abandonment.
- Feedback from the adult.
- Safety incident or near miss.

Do not request photos of children or continuous recording. Troubleshooting means follow temporary policy.

## Interviews

- Onboarding: expectations and restrictions.
- Weekly: five minutes maximum; what worked and what was left over.
- Closing: final interview about usefulness, trust, and burden.

## Interim success criteria

- 70% or more of initiated activities reach a useful closure.
- Median closing time of less than 20 seconds for three children.
- Less than 15% finish “too easy” or “couldn't” due to poor assignment, after calibration.
- No critical security or privacy incidents.
- The majority of adults affirm that the preparation was equal to or less than expected.
- In the functional phase with `VS-04`, inference corrections are decreased while still being available. This criterion is not evaluated during the first dry runs or with `VS-01–03` alone.
- The founder can sustain use for eight weeks without preparation and documentation becoming a disproportionate burden.
- Friendly families provide real sessions with two or three children, in addition to differences in age, experience and style of accompaniment.

Thresholds must be approved before the pilot and do not replace qualitative analysis.
